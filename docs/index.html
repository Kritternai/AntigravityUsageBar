Built for Google Antigravity

Zero manual configuration. Instant local telemetry.

Zero-Config Auto-Discovery

No browser DevTools or cookie copying needed. Automatically discovers your local Antigravity language_server process, port, and CSRF token.

All Antigravity Models

Monitors remaining quota and exact reset times across Gemini 4 Argon, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Opus 5.5, and GPT-OSS 120B.

Local Token Telemetry

Scans ~/.gemini/antigravity/brain transcripts locally to aggregate Today and All-Time input, output, and cache-read tokens.

Smart Threshold Alerts

Receive native macOS notifications when your active model quota crosses 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90% utilization.

Pace Marker

Visual tick marker on every bar showing where your usage should sit based on how far into the 5-hour or 7-day reset window you are.

100% Local & Private

Talks strictly to 127.0.0.1 and local files. Zero external servers, zero telemetry, and fully open source under the MIT License.

Frequently Asked Questions

How AntigravityUsageBar works under the hood

How does AntigravityUsageBar fetch my quota without cookies?
When Google Antigravity is running on your Mac, its background language_server listens on a localhost port. AntigravityUsageBar finds the running process, reads its ephemeral port and --csrf_token, and queries LanguageServerService/GetUserStatus over 127.0.0.1.
macOS says the app cannot be opened or is from an unidentified developer?
Because this is a free open-source build signed ad-hoc, right-click AntigravityUsageBar.app and choose Open, or run xattr -cr /Applications/AntigravityUsageBar.app in Terminal.
What keyboard shortcut toggles the popup?
Press Cmd + Shift + U (⌘⇧U) from anywhere on macOS. It uses ⌘⇧U so it can run side-by-side with ClaudeUsageBar (⌘U) without conflict.
